Our story

Building a more accessible path to cognitive risk awareness

AlzAware began with a simple question: what if people could better understand cognitive risk before the concern becomes a crisis?

Our team is developing a responsible digital prototype that helps users interpret model-based risk signals without replacing clinical judgment.

Mission

Make early cognitive risk awareness easier to access, easier to understand, and easier to act on responsibly.

Mission, vision, values

Designed for awareness, not diagnosis

Our mission is to create a public-facing risk-awareness tool that helps older adults, caregivers, and care organizations recognize when a professional evaluation may be worth considering.

Mission

Lower the barrier to early cognitive risk awareness through a simple digital assessment and interpretable model output.

Vision

Create a scalable triage layer that helps communities identify cognitive risk earlier and direct limited clinical resources more effectively.

Values

Accessibility, transparency, responsible AI, privacy, and respect for the difference between risk awareness and medical diagnosis.

Who benefits

A tool for families, clinics, and senior-care settings

AlzAware is designed for people who may not have immediate access to formal neuropsychological testing. It can support conversations between users, families, caregivers, and healthcare professionals.

Public health contribution

By making first-step risk awareness inexpensive and scalable, AlzAware has the potential to reduce delayed evaluation, improve care navigation, and help organizations prioritize follow-up for higher-risk individuals.
