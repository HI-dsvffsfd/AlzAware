Business overview

A scalable early-awareness layer for cognitive health

AlzAware’s long-term business potential comes from serving organizations that need low-cost, repeatable cognitive risk triage: senior-care facilities, primary-care clinics, and community health programs.

B2B

Clinics and senior-care organizations

SaaS

Recurring subscription model

Scale

Low marginal cost per assessment

Target customers

Organizations that need affordable cognitive screening support

AlzAware is not positioned as a hospital diagnostic product. Its strongest early market is risk triage: helping care teams and community programs identify who may benefit from further professional evaluation.

Senior-care facilities

Regular resident check-ins can help staff and families notice cognitive risk signals earlier and document changes over time.

Primary-care clinics

A short digital tool can help busy clinics prioritize conversations and referrals when formal testing capacity is limited.

Community health programs

Public health groups can use an accessible tool to expand cognitive awareness in underserved or rural communities.

Economic value

Lower-cost triage before higher-cost evaluation

Formal neuropsychological testing and specialist evaluation require trained personnel, appointment time, and clinical infrastructure. AlzAware can provide an inexpensive first-step layer that helps direct attention toward higher-risk individuals.

This creates economic value by improving prioritization, supporting early conversations, and reducing avoidable friction in the care pathway.

Illustrative revenue scenario

A $3 per resident per month subscription across 100,000 monitored users would represent approximately $3.6M in annual recurring revenue.

Revenue model

Subscription-based, with future research partnerships

Phase 1: Public prototype

Use the website, demo, and app prototype to validate usability, messaging, and responsible communication.

Phase 2: Organization pilots

Offer low-cost subscriptions to senior-care facilities and clinics that need recurring cognitive risk check-ins.

Phase 3: Research expansion

Explore partnerships for clinical-trial pre-screening, longitudinal monitoring, and anonymized model improvement.

Social and economic contribution

AlzAware aims to help older adults, caregivers, and community health organizations act earlier, reduce uncertainty, and use limited healthcare resources more efficiently.
