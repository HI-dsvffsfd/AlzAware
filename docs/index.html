Digital cognitive risk triage

AlzAware

AlzAware is a student-built cognitive risk reference platform designed to make early Alzheimer’s risk awareness more accessible, interpretable, and responsible.

The prototype combines self-reported health information with a machine-learning score, then compares that score with AD and non-AD reference populations.

Why it matters

A clearer first step before formal evaluation

Many people notice memory or attention changes long before they receive a professional evaluation. AlzAware addresses the gap between casual concern and clinical testing by offering a low-cost, educational reference tool.

1

Accessible

Runs through a browser-based assessment and requires no specialized equipment, making it suitable for public outreach, caregivers, and community settings.

2

Interpretable

Instead of presenting a diagnosis, the platform contextualizes each score against reference populations so users can understand relative risk.

3

Responsible

The product is framed as risk awareness and triage support, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

Platform vision

From one-time screening to longitudinal awareness

The long-term vision is to help older adults, families, clinics, and senior-care organizations track cognitive risk signals over time. Repeated monthly assessments could make subtle changes easier to notice and discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.

A

Answer

Users complete a short health and demographic questionnaire.

B

Score

The model generates a reference score from processed predictors.

C

Compare

The result is compared with AD and non-AD reference populations.

Educational use only

AlzAware is not a medical device and does not diagnose Alzheimer’s disease. It is designed to support awareness, conversation, and appropriate follow-up with healthcare professionals.
