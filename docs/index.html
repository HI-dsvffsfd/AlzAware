Our Goal

1

Help older adults become more aware of their cognitive health risks.

2

Provide powerful, low-cost or no-cost tools to communities.

3

Use new knowledge and technology to benefit people around the world.

Our Mission

To make early cognitive risk awareness easier to access by transforming simple demographic and health-history information into an interpretable machine-learning reference score that supports earlier conversations, smarter triage, and responsible clinical follow-up.

Prediction

Providing an early cognitive-risk reference using simple demographic and health information.

Cost-Efficiency

Reducing the need for costly formal evaluations while helping clinics and care organizations prioritize limited clinical resources.

Accessibility

Making advanced cognitive-health insights more accessible by transforming demographic and health-history information into a machine-learning risk score trained on a large cognitive-health dataset.
