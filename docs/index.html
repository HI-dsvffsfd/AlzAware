Who we are

Our Team

AlzAware is developed by a student team focused on making early cognitive risk awareness more accessible, interpretable, and responsible. Our project brings together interests in health technology, machine learning, public health, and user-centered design.

The team includes [member 1], [member 2], and [member 3], with guidance from [coach or mentor]. Together, we are building a digital prototype that translates complex risk-model outputs into a clearer reference result for older adults, caregivers, and care organizations.

Our goal is not to replace clinicians, but to create a low-cost first step that helps people recognize when professional cognitive evaluation may be worth considering.

Platform vision

From one-time screening to longitudinal awareness

The long-term vision is to help older adults, families, clinics, and senior-care organizations track cognitive risk signals over time. Repeated monthly assessments could make subtle changes easier to notice and discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.
