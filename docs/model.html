Model overview

Predictive model for AD

AlzAware uses a pre-trained machine-learning model to calculate a cognitive risk reference score from structured health and demographic information.

Model built with

  • More than 150,000 participants
  • 24 predictors
  • Cutting-edge AI approach: XGBoost classifier

Output interpretation

Output interpretation

All participants' risk scores are predicted using the final model, and the score distributions for the two study samples, AD and non-AD, are visualized separately.

The larger the score, the higher the model-estimated risk.

Your predicted risk score is compared with the two reference samples to support decisions about whether professional cognitive evaluation may be worth considering.
